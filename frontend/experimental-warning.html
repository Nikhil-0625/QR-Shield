How to read this demo

A warning does not confirm phishing. No warning does not guarantee safety. A SAFE risk classification is also not a guarantee of safety.

The exact experimental warning threshold is 0.40144790130467084, inclusive. It applies to the candidate phishing score, not to the 0–100 hybrid risk score. Candidate scores are not independently calibrated probabilities of harm.

At this validation-selected threshold, measured phishing recall was about 90%, with a 19.40% false-positive rate: approximately one in five benign validation examples received a warning. About one in ten phishing examples was missed. These are validation results, not guarantees for future scans.

Dataset version and upstream licensing remain unverified. The optional prediction form connects only to the isolated local experimental API. The production scanner remains separate.
